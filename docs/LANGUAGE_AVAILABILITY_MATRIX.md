## How to read it

* **Interface ready** = all ten essential labels (navigation, search, language) exist in that language. It says nothing about quality: see *Interface text by* and *Confidence*.
* **MACHINE_TRANSLATED** interface text was generated by an AI model and has **not** been reviewed by a native speaker. The app shows a notice in every such language. *low* confidence means labels the model was unsure of were left in English instead of being guessed.
* **PROJECT_AUTHORED** = written for this project in its two working languages; not independently reviewed.
* **Owner summaries** are explanations written by the project owner. They are not the text of the law.
* **Official text**, **OCR awaiting review**, **Machine-translated law** and **Human-verified** count rows of `article_texts`. Machine-translated law is 0 by design: the project does not machine-translate the Constitution.
* For a language without content the app shows the English (or Gujarati) content and says so; the assistant accepts the question, recognises the script and article number, and answers in English with a notice.
